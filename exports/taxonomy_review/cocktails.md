# 🥃 Camden Reserve (Maple Old Fashioned Batch)

## 🧪 Batch Size
~1.75L bottle (filled ~3/4 full)

---

## 🍶 Ingredients

- Bourbon: **900 ml (3.8 cups)**
- Maple Syrup: **120 ml (1/2 cup)**
- Angostura Bitters: **25–30 dashes (~1.25–1.5 oz)**
- Orange Bitters: **15–20 dashes (~0.75–1 oz)**

---

## 🛠️ Build Steps

1. Add bourbon to a large mixing container  
2. Add maple syrup  
3. Add Angostura bitters  
4. Add orange bitters  
5. Stir thoroughly until fully combined  
6. Taste and adjust if needed:
   - Too strong → add a small amount of maple syrup  
   - Too sweet → add a few more dashes of bitters  
7. Funnel into bottle  
8. Refrigerate  

---

## 🧊 Serve

1. Add a large ice cube to glass  
2. Pour cocktail over ice  
3. Express a **tangerine or orange peel** over the drink  
4. Optional: drop peel into glass  

---

## 🧠 Notes

- Do NOT add fruit to the bottle  
- Shake bottle lightly before serving  
- This improves after resting overnight

# 🍋 Springdale Spritz (Batch Base)

## 🧪 Batch Size
~1.75L bottle (filled ~3/4 full)

---

## 🍶 Ingredients

- Vodka (or Gin): **600 ml (2.5 cups)**
- Fresh Lemon Juice: **180 ml (3/4 cup)**
- Simple Syrup: **180 ml (3/4 cup)**
- Orange Bitters (optional): **6–10 dashes**

---

## 🛠️ Build Steps

1. Add vodka/gin to mixing container  
2. Add lemon juice  
3. Add simple syrup  
4. Add orange bitters (optional)  
5. Stir thoroughly  
6. Taste and adjust:
   - Too tart → add a bit more simple syrup  
   - Too sweet → add a splash more lemon juice  
7. Funnel into bottle  
8. Refrigerate  

---

## 🧊 Serve

1. Fill glass with ice  
2. Pour cocktail base (~3/4 of glass)  
3. Top with **club soda**  
4. Gently stir  
5. Garnish with **lemon peel**

---

## 🧠 Notes

- Shake bottle lightly before serving  
- Add soda only when serving (not in batch)  
- Best served very cold  

---

# 🔥 Final Tips

- Prep garnishes ahead of time (peels > slices)
- Use large ice for Camden Reserve
- Keep everything cold
- Bring a small towel for bottle handling
